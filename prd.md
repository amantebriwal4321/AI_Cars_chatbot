# Product Requirements Document: AutoExpert Chatbot

## Vision
To provide a specialized, highly accurate, and helpful AI chatbot dedicated entirely to the domain of cars, automotives, and vehicle maintenance, equipped with multimodal vision capabilities to analyze images of car parts or issues.

## Target User
Car enthusiasts, mechanics, daily drivers needing maintenance advice, and anyone curious about automotive engineering, history, or troubleshooting.

## Must-Have Features
- Chat interface tailored for automotive topics.
- Image upload feature allowing users to upload pictures of cars, engine bays, dashboard lights, or car parts for identification and analysis.
- Strict domain adherence: the bot must politely redirect non-automotive queries.
- Helpful, non-paranoid system instruction: it can answer general automotive queries and give maintenance advice with appropriate disclaimers, refusing only truly harmful instructions or off-topic asks.

## Non-Goals
- Real-time OBD2 diagnostics integration.
- Booking mechanic appointments.
- E-commerce for car parts.

## Success Criteria
- Accurately answers car-related questions.
- Successfully identifies car parts and issues from uploaded images.
- Rejects non-car images and non-car text prompts gracefully.
